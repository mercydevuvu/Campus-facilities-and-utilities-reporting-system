
Target Users# Campus Electricity

1. Project Overview

The Campus Electricity Monitoring and Reporting System is a proposed digital 
platform designed to help students report electricity outages and receive
updates about power availability across different campus locations.

Electricity is important to students for studying, charging devices, accessing 
the internet and completing assignments. However, irregular electricity 
supply and unexpected outages often disrupt these activities.

Students also struggle to get accurate information about when electricity will 
be restored. They sometimes experience unexpected power interruptions 
without receiving reliable information and often depend on friends 
or hostel representatives for updates.

Students: Need reliable electricity information to support their academic activities.

HostelResidents: Need updates about electricity availability in their hostels.
Lecturers: May experience interruptions during online teaching and other academic activities.

Lecturers:May experience interruptions during online teaching and other academic activities.

2. Existing Solutions and Their Limitations

Students currently manage electricity problems by:

- Asking roommates and classmates.
- Using power banks.
- Moving to locations with electricity.
- Waiting for power to return.

3. Limitations

- Some updates may be inaccurate or outdated.
- Students may not know where to report electricity outages.
- Information is often shared informally between students.

4. Student Interviews

We conducted interviews with students on campus to understand their experiences
with electricity supply.

First Interview

Question:How does poor electricity supply affect your studies?

Response:Sometimes I cannot charge my laptop or read comfortably at 
night because there is no electricity. The power outage also affects me.

Second Interview

Question:What do you normally do when electricity goes off?

Response:I usually ask my roommates if there is any information that was passed earlier, or ask my friends in other hostels whether they are experiencing the same problem.

Third Interview

**Question:** Would a platform for reporting electricity outages be useful?

Response:Yes, because students could easily check the electricity situation instead of asking different people, and we could be informed earlier.

5. Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is a **Campus Electricity Monitoring and Reporting System**.

The platform will allow students to:

- Report electricity outages.
- View existing outage reports.
- Check the latest available electricity updates across campus.
- Receive updates when reports are reviewed or electricity is reported to have returned.

The main goal is to make electricity reporting easier for students and provide a central location for electricity updates.

6. Minimum Viable Product (MVP) and Main Features

The first version of the system will focus on the following features:

Report Outages
Students can report electricity problems by selecting their campus location, such as Main Campus or Iperu Campus.

View Reports
Students can view existing outage reports submitted by other users.

Check Electricity Status
The system displays the latest reported electricity status in different campus locations.

Receive Updates
Students can view updates when reports are reviewed or electricity is reported to 
have returned.

7. Information Required for Reporting

Students will provide the following information when reporting an outage:

- Campus location.
- Date and time of outage.
- A short description of the problem.

8. Expected Outcome
The system is expected to make electricity information more accessible, 
reduce confusion among students and provide campus management with 
organised reports that can help them understand recurring electricity problems.

9. Conclusion
The Campus Electricity Monitoring and Reporting System aims to provide a simple
and organised way for students to report electricity outages and access 
reliable updates across different campus locations.
