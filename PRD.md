PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)
Project Title

**Campus Electricity Monitoring and Reporting System**
Project Overview

The Campus Electricity Monitoring and Reporting System is a proposed digital platform designed to help students and other members of the university community report electricity outages and access information about electricity availability across different campus locations.

The platform will provide a central place where users can submit outage reports, view reports submitted by others, and check the latest reported electricity status for different locations.

The purpose of the project is to reduce the difficulty of finding reliable information about electricity interruptions and make communication about power availability easier within the university community.

 Problem Statement

Unreliable electricity supply can affect students' academic activities and everyday routines on campus. Students may experience difficulties charging laptops and mobile phones, studying at night, completing assignments, and carrying out other activities that depend on electricity.

Currently, students may have to ask roommates, classmates, or friends in other hostels to find out whether electricity is available in their locations.

This informal method of sharing information can be inconvenient because information may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable.

There is a need for a centralized platform where students can report electricity outages and access updates about reported electricity conditions across campus.

 Background and Context

Electricity is important to students because many academic and personal activities depend on it.

Students use electricity to charge electronic devices, access online learning materials, complete assignments, study, and communicate.

When electricity becomes unavailable, students may need to find alternative ways to continue their activities.

The proposed system focuses on improving access to information about electricity availability rather than directly controlling or restoring electricity supply.

Target Users

The primary users of the proposed system include:

- **Students: Students who need information about electricity availability to plan their academic and personal activities.
- **Hostel Residents:Students living in university hostels who experience electricity interruptions and need updates about their locations.
- **Lecturers:** Academic staff who may also need information about electricity availability when planning activities that depend on electricity.

 Research Findings

Based on the three interviews:

- Electricity interruptions can affect students' academic activities.
- Students currently depend on informal communication to find out about electricity availability.
- Students interviewed expressed interest in a platform that makes electricity information easier to access.
- A centralized reporting system could reduce the need to repeatedly ask different people for updates.

**Research limitation:** Only three interviews have been conducted so far. These findings provide initial insights and should not be treated as representative of the entire university population. Further interviews and surveys are needed.

## 7. Existing Solutions and Their Limitations

Students currently use several informal methods to obtain information about electricity availability.

Existing Methods

1. Asking roommates or hostel neighbours.
2. Contacting friends in other hostels.
3. Waiting for electricity to return.
4. Using power banks or other alternative charging options.
5. Moving to locations where electricity is available.

Limitations

- Information may not reach everyone.
- Students may receive delayed or inaccurate updates.
- There is no centralized place to view reported outages.
- Students may need to contact several people before getting useful information.
- Information about electricity availability may become outdated.

 Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is a Campus Electricity Monitoring and Reporting System.

It will provide a centralized digital platform where users can report electricity outages and view information submitted by other users.

Users will be able to select their campus location, provide a short description of the outage, and submit a report.

Other users will be able to view existing reports and check the latest reported electricity status for different locations.

The system will help improve communication and access to electricity-related information within the university community.

Product Goals and Objectives

The main goals of the system are:

1. Make reporting electricity outages easier.
2. Provide a central location for viewing outage reports.
3. Help students access electricity-related information.
4. Reduce dependence on informal communication.
5. Improve awareness of reported electricity conditions across campus.

. Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

The first version of the system will focus on the most important features needed to solve the identified problem.
 MVP Features

1. **Outage Reporting:** Users can submit electricity outage reports.
2. **Location Selection:** Users can identify the affected campus location.
3. **Report Description:** Users can provide a short explanation of the reported outage.
4. **View Reports:** Users can view reports submitted by other users.
5. **Electricity Status:** Users can check the latest reported electricity status for a selected location.
6. **Report Updates:** Users can view updates when a report is reviewed or when electricity is reported to have returned.

Features such as advanced analytics, automated notifications, and integration with official electricity infrastructure will not be part of the initial MVP.

Functional Requirements

The system should:

- Allow users to submit electricity outage reports.
- Allow users to select the affected campus location.
- Record the date and time of each report.
- Allow users to include a short description.
- Store submitted reports.
- Display previously submitted reports.
- Allow users to view the latest reported status of a location.
- Allow reports to be updated when their status changes.
- Distinguish between active, resolved, and unverified reports.

Reporting an Outage

1. The user opens the platform.
2. The user selects the affected campus location.
3. The user enters a short description of the electricity problem.
4. The user submits the report.
5. The system records the report with its date and time.
6. The report becomes available for other users to view.

 Checking Electricity Status

1. The user opens the platform.
2. The user selects a campus location.
3. The system displays available reports for that location.
4. The user checks the latest reported electricity status.

Updating a Report

1. An authorized user reviews an existing report.
2. The report status is updated when reliable information becomes available.
3. Other users can view the updated status.

User Information
- User ID
- Name or username

 Electricity Report Information
- Report ID

 Status Information

- Update description, where applicable.

Success Criteria

The initial version of the system will be considered successful if:

- Users can successfully submit electricity outage reports.
- Reports are stored and displayed correctly.
- Users can identify the location associated with each report.
- Users can view the latest available reported electricity status.
- Report statuses can be updated.
- Users find the platform easier to use than relying entirely on informal communication.

 Risks and Limitations

Inaccurate Reports
Users may submit incorrect or misleading information.

**Possible Solution:** Introduce report review and verification procedures.

### Outdated Information
A report may remain visible after electricity has returned.

**Possible Solution:** Provide report status updates and timestamps.

### Limited User Participation
The system may not be useful if very few students submit reports.

**Possible Solution:** Encourage students to participate and make reporting simple.

Internet Access
Users need internet access to access the platform.

Possible Solution: Keep the platform lightweight and accessible on mobile devices.

 Verification Challenges
The system may not have direct access to official electricity supply information.

**Possible Solution:** Clearly distinguish user-submitted reports from verified updates.

 Future Improvements

Possible improvements after the MVP include:

- Push notifications when new reports are submitted.
- Location-based filtering.
- An administrator dashboard.
- Report verification by authorized users.
- Electricity outage history and statistics.
- Support for additional campus locations.
- Integration with official university electricity information systems, if access becomes available.

 Conclusion

The Campus Electricity Monitoring and Reporting System aims to address the difficulty students experience when trying to obtain information about electricity availability across campus.

Initial interviews suggest that students rely on informal communication to understand electricity conditions and that a centralized reporting platform could make this process easier.

By focusing on outage reporting, location-based information, report viewing, and status updates, the proposed MVP provides a practical starting point for solving the identified problem.

Further research and user feedback will help improve the system before and during development.
